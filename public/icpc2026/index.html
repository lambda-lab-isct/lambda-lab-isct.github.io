Team Introductions — ICPC 2026 Asia Yokohama Regional

Each team is asked to submit two items: a Team Introduction Document (plain text) and a Team Introduction Slide. Please read the notes below and use the links to submit.

Team Introduction Document

Deadline: Tuesday, November 3, 2026, 17:00 JST (UTC+9)

  • At most 35 lines, each at most 75 characters.
  • Printable ASCII characters only (SPACE, letters, digits, and symbols). No Japanese, Chinese, or emoji. A TAB is expanded to SPACEs.
  • If needed, explain how to pronounce your team name.
  • You are encouraged to mention your university, city, and team members.
  • The text is typeset verbatim in a monospace font, so ASCII art may not appear as intended.
  • The submission page checks your text and suggests a corrected version if it does not meet the rules.
Submit Team Introduction Document

Team Introduction Slide

Deadline: Tuesday, November 10, 2026

  • Submit your slide through the Google Form below.
  • The first field of the form may be labeled in Japanese; it asks for your email address.
  • If you have any trouble with the form, you may send your slide by email to icpc2026@lambda.comp.isct.ac.jp instead.
